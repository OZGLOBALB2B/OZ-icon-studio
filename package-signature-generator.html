How to add your signature

Outlook

  1. Click "Copy signature".
  2. In Outlook: File > Options > Mail > Signatures.
  3. Create a new signature and paste (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V).

Gmail

  1. Click "Copy signature".
  2. Gmail Settings > See all settings > Signature > paste into the box.
  3. Click "Save Changes" at the bottom of the page.
